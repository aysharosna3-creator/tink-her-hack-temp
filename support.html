❓ Common Questions

How do I cancel my booking?

Go to Ticket Booking → My Tickets → Cancel. Refund will be processed in 3–5 working days.

Can I change my seat after booking?

Yes! Visit Seat Booking page up to 2 hours before departure to modify your seat selection.

What if my bus is delayed?

Use Bus Tracking to check real-time status. If delay exceeds 30 minutes, you're eligible for a partial refund.
